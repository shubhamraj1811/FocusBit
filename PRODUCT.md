# FOCUSBIT

## Core Idea

A minimal, distraction-free Android study stopwatch designed to help users know exactly how much time they spend studying without turning their phone into another source of distraction.

The core philosophy is:

> **Measure your study time without becoming another distraction.**

The app should feel like a calm digital study clock rather than a conventional productivity app.

## 1. Focus Mode

The primary experience of the app.

### Study Flow

**Open App → Start Session → Optional Category → Stopwatch Starts → Study → Pause or Save**

When a session starts:

- The screen becomes minimal and distraction-free.
- A large digital stopwatch occupies the screen.
- The interface resembles a classic digital stopwatch.
- No feed, unnecessary information, motivational content, or distracting elements.
- The user simply puts the phone down and studies.

### Focus Mode Controls

**Pause**

- Temporarily pauses the current session.
- Intended for short interruptions or breaks.
- The same session can later be resumed.

**Save**

- Ends the current session.
- Saves the completed study time as a record.

## 2. Optional Study Slots / Categories

Users are **not required** to categorize their sessions.

When starting a session, they can choose:

- DSA
- Android
- Semester
- Default
- 
    - Add New Slot

Users can create their own custom slots such as:

- GATE
- Projects
- Mathematics
- Coding
- etc.

### Default Usage

A user can simply study without choosing a category:

**Session 1 — 2h 10m**

**Session 2 — 1h 40m**

### Categorized Usage

**DSA — 1h 10m**

### Mixed Usage

Users can freely combine both approaches:

**DSA — 1h 10m**

**Session 2 — 4h**

**GATE — 2h**

Categories are therefore a **tool, not a requirement**.

The workflow should remain as frictionless as possible.

---

## 3. Study Records

Every saved session becomes part of the user's study history.

A record can contain:

- Session/category name
- Duration
- Date
- Session sequence where applicable

This creates a long-term personal record of study time.

---

## 4. Daily Snapshot

The app provides a simple snapshot of recent study activity.

Example:

**Yesterday**

**4h 32m studied**

3 sessions

DSA — 1h 10m

Android — 2h 02m

Session 3 — 1h 20m

The previous day's snapshot is visible on the main/home dashboard.

The purpose is simply to show the user:

> **What did I actually accomplish yesterday?**

---

## 5. Streak System

The app includes a minimal study streak.

Example:

**6 day streak**

The streak exists to encourage consistency and give users a reason to return regularly.

However, it should remain subtle and non-toxic.

The app should encourage:

> **"Nice, you've been consistent."**

rather than:

> **"You failed your streak."**

Streaks should never dominate the product experience.

---

## 6. Historical Study Data

The app stores completed study sessions over time.

Users can review their study history across:

- Days
- Weeks
- Months
- Longer periods

The exact long-term retention period can be finalized during product development, with possibilities including 3, 6, or 12 months and beyond.

---

## 7. Snapshots & Monthly Reports

The Data/History area will provide:

- Historical study snapshots
- Monthly reports
- Study-time summaries
- Previous sessions
- Category-based history where applicable

The exact statistics and presentation will be defined later.

---

## 8. Two Main App Modes

### Focus Mode

**Purpose: Study**

- Full-screen
- Minimal
- Digital stopwatch
- Pause
- Save
- No distracting content

The app should essentially disappear while the user is studying.

### Dashboard Mode

**Purpose: Understand your study**

Contains the user's:

- Recent snapshot
- Study history
- Streak
- Reports
- Session records
- Category information

The dashboard can provide more information because the user is no longer in a study session.

## 9. V1 Scope

The first version intentionally remains minimal.

### Included

- Study sessions
- Full-screen stopwatch
- Pause/resume
- Save completed sessions
- Default sessions
- Custom study slots/categories
- Mixed categorized + uncategorized sessions
- Daily snapshots
- Study streak
- Historical data
- Snapshots
- Monthly reports
- Focus Mode
- Dashboard Mode

### Not Included in V1

- Study goals
- Daily time targets
- Authentication
- User accounts
- Profiles
- Social features
- Leaderboards
- AI coaching
- Task management
- Notes
- Unnecessary gamification
- Feeds or distracting content

---

## Product Philosophy

The app follows one central rule:

> **Every interaction that does not directly help the user study or understand their study time should be questioned.**

The ideal experience is:

**Sit down → Open app → Start → Study → Pause/Resume or Save → Done.**

The app measures the user's behavior without trying to control it.

### Product Identity

**A clock for your focus, not another distraction.**
